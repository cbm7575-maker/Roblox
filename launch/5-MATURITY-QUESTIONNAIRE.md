the average.

**Expected label: Mild, but it's now a judgment call. Read this before you answer.** Roblox's
content-maturity guide (as summarized by a web search; I couldn't open roblox.com from here) puts
violence that is *implied or unrealistic* under Mild, e.g. bodies that disappear when their health
reaches zero. It puts *realistic-looking* violence or death under Moderate. Repeated mild violence is
still Mild. Neon Coast's guns fit the Mild description: neon tracers, no blood, and the stock Roblox
break-apart-and-respawn death. But they are realistic-shaped guns used on other players. Answer the
questionnaire honestly for the most intense moment, which is one player shooting another until they
fall apart. If it asks whether weapons are realistic, the answer is yes (pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle
shapes); if it asks whether injuries or blood are shown, the answer is no.

What each label means for you:
- **Mild (9+):** your PRD's target. It still reaches 9-15 year-olds.
- **Moderate (13+):** loses the under-13 audience.
- If Roblox gives Moderate and you want Mild back, the lever is the guns: remove them, or make them
  clearly non-realistic (water blasters, paint, foam).

Check Roblox's current wording at create.roblox.com (Production > Promotion > Content maturity) before
you submit.

**Also from the PRD (account to-do):** turn on ID verification and 2-step verification. Both are
needed before a game can reach players under 16. Do it on TCTRADES_CHRIS; if NightShift Dev is a
group, Roblox may check the group owner's account, so if that's a different account, do it there too.
