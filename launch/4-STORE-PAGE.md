# 4. Store page (Theft Miami Coast)

Everything here describes features that are in the code today. Naming rule (from your Oct 14 notes):
the game is THEFT MIAMI COAST and the city is Port Solana. Never add Grand, Auto or a number, and keep
the big series' name (and Vice City, Leonida, Rockstar, real car brands) out of the title, description,
tags, thumbnails and ads.

Three Roblox rules shape this page (checked Nov 2, 2026):
- **Keep it fit for all ages.** Even if the game is 16+, the description, thumbnails and videos must suit
  a general audience.
- **Never mention hanging out.** Roblox treats a game as a social hangout if its title or description
  refers to one, and a hangout with bedrooms is 18+. That's why the car meet isn't listed below.
- **Stay unique.** Ads for games that aren't unique can end up with no ad spend at all, and listings
  that closely resemble existing games can rank lower.

## Ads (Ads Manager)

An ad is a sponsored thumbnail on the Home page and in search results. Roblox's docs list no keyword
targeting, so the old keyword list is gone from this page.

- **Thumbnails:** up to 10 per campaign, shown at 16:9. The ones above work, and they get moderated
  first (Roblox aims for 24 hours).
- **Audience for a new game:** All Players or New Players. Recent and Lapsed Players need 10,000 and
  20,000 of those players first.
- **Targeting:** location, age, gender, genre and device type. Set age to match who can actually play
  (16+ on Route 1 in launch/5). The docs don't say Roblox does this for you.
- **Budget:** daily or lifetime. Roblox sets the bids itself. There's no published price per play.
- **Paying:** cards are for ages 18+. A first-time card is charged $5 when you submit, plus a $1
  temporary hold. Turning Robux into ad credits can't be undone.
