sunset fill over the striped sun) above a dusk coast with palms, police lights and a getaway car. It

## Thumbnails (you capture these; they must show the real game)

16:9, 1920 x 1080. Ideas:
1. Sunset chase on the causeway: a car with police lights behind, palm silhouettes.
2. A crew at THE BIG SCORE vault, or the armored van run.
3. Flamingo Beach at night: neon and the Ferris wheel on its pier.

How: in Studio, press Play and hide the HUD (Explorer > Players > you > PlayerGui, untick Enabled on
each ScreenGui). Set the time of day, frame the shot, and press Win+Shift+S.

**Video thumbnail:** 15-30 seconds of a real chase. Record the Roblox window with Win+G (Xbox Game
Bar). Gameplay video autoplays on the Roblox home page, so it's worth doing.
