## Thumbnails (you capture these; they must show the real game)

Shot list from the PRD. 16:9, 1920 x 1080.

1. Sunset chase on the causeway: a robber's car with a cop car's lights behind, palm silhouettes.
2. A crew cracking the Coastal Bank vault.
3. Flamingo Beach at night: neon, the Ferris wheel (Solana Sky Wheel).

How: in Studio press Play, hide the HUD (Explorer > Players > you > PlayerGui, untick Enabled on
each ScreenGui), set the time of day you want, frame the shot, and press Win+Shift+S. Or, in a
local Claude session on your PC with the Studio MCP, I can place cameras and take these
screenshots myself.

**Video thumbnail:** 15-30 seconds of a real chase. Record the Roblox window with Win+G (Xbox Game
Bar). Gameplay video autoplays on the Roblox home page, so it's worth doing.
